# Inspirations

- **Flipism:** the user's supplied concept of settling choices through a randomizer, such as dice or a coin. This skill applies it to viable choices delegated within a task.
- [Charles Bukowski, Roll the Dice](https://hellopoetry.com/poem/68266/roll-the-dice): interpreted here as commitment to carrying the chosen work through difficulty. The poem is linked and paraphrased as an inspiration; its text is not included or relicensed.
- **User-selected behavior:** choose a viable path and carry it through, rather than repeatedly randomizing the whole task or stopping at a suggestion.
- Created: 2026-10-13.
