# Tutorial reproduction and style transfer

Read when the user asks to follow a complete tutorial, reproduce its final artwork, or apply the demonstrated treatment to a new subject. Follow the actual lesson sequence and distinguish source observation, method replay, visual fidelity and independent editing.

## Establish the source and target state

Read captions and inspect the demonstrated actions, including accelerated sequences, mirrored views, revisions after the first declared final, brush-editor controls and saved layer structure. Keep exact reviewed intervals and observation cadence. Sampled frames cover selected states; they do not establish continuous gesture/pressure observation. Mirrors of one lesson remain one source.

Identify the published final target and its dimensions/profile. Match crop and viewing orientation before measuring. Keep missing presets, numerical controls, offscreen references and unrecorded decisions as explicit gaps. A reconstructed list of steps is not an exact brush-event replay.

## Reproduce the demonstrated method

Before construction, map source stages to actual native operations and checkpoints. Preserve observed order and corrections. Calibrate the demonstrated brush behavior, pressure response, tip ratio/angle, spacing, blends, adjustments and spatial masks. If current tools cannot perform a required step, record the substitute and its effect on the 1:1 requirement.

When the teacher consolidates layers, retain a tutorial-faithful branch with pre-consolidation checkpoints and a separately assessed owner-preserving adaptation. Hand-authored filled contours and per-item rendering may be useful construction, but they are substitutions when the lesson builds volumes through freehand underpainting and merged paint-over. Naming eight stages does not establish that those operations happened.

Resolve one important material region before propagating its procedure. Compare the actual source's silhouette, light/shadow volumes, transition width, mark direction and edge breaks at equal scale. If a painted curl becomes alternating flat ribbons, change the volume and transition procedure before adding more parallel marks. A plausible contour with decorative texture has not reproduced the source's material.

## Calibrate boundaries and focus

Test whether a contour intentionally crosses its base silhouette before inheriting alpha. An upper eyelid painted on an inherited-alpha tone layer can lose the part above the eye fill. Keep the lid under the eye's semantic owner while permitting the required boundary extension; compare clipped/unclipped proof images and the character composite.

Test native continuous-path and segmented-stroke behavior separately. Resetting a brush for every segment can expose round stamp caps and produce scalloped bands. A smooth native filled ribbon removes that artifact but can introduce a graphic treatment; judge its appearance against the lesson rather than accepting the tool change as a style match.

For capsule chains, flattened curved shading or a material mismatch, use the hair/cloth branches in [materials-and-light.md](materials-and-light.md). Test object-bound flow and local tones in a saved limited region before extending a dense procedure.

For swollen rims, newly exposed gaps, stationary fragments or invisible glazes, use the [diagnostic and native-operation checks](drawing-decisions.md#test-a-correction-before-extending-it). Compare actual paint footprints, receiver ownership, layer order and projected filter fringe as well as the source appearance.

For depth of field, match the teacher's focus regions and receding edges. A full-white filter mask attached only to an environment group softens the background, but does not reproduce a mask that protects character/props and selectively softens their distant contours. Inspect the saved mask/configuration and the composite before asserting parity. Pixel-radius settings apply at the demonstrated output dimensions.

## Transfer and acceptance

Derive a recipe from observed decisions and actual reconstruction results. Freeze the recipe and a materially different subject/pose/props/setting before constructing the transfer image. Preserve proportion language, value economy, environmental color/light, directional marks and focal edges; record the content changes and method deviations.

Evaluate reproduction, method fidelity, editability, dependencies, persistence and transfer separately. Inspect actual move/remove/recolor/reshape variants and reopened results. An iris translation exercises placement, not iris shape modification. A strap/parcel translation without repaired grip, shadows and attachments leaves dependency requirements unmet. Surface marks stored together remain aggregates even when their parent is independently editable.

A failed reproduction or transfer is a useful observed result, not support for claiming the style was learned. Preserve candidates and diagnoses, identify the unresolved procedure and report the exact requested result still unfulfilled.
